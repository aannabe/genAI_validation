# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-007
**Revision:** 2025
**Status:** Current
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Supersedes:** Remote Work Policy (2022 revision)

---

## Eligibility
All employees are remote-first. Employees may work from their home location within the United States, or from an Insurellm office in San Francisco, New York, Austin, Chicago, or Denver.

## Home Office Stipend
Employees receive a home office stipend of $100 per month, paid with regular payroll, to cover internet and workspace costs. A one-time equipment allowance of $750 is available within the first 30 days of employment.

## Availability
Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of 10 AM - 3 PM in their local time zone.

## Security
Remote work must comply with the Information Security Policy, including MFA and use of company-managed devices.
