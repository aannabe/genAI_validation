# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-004
**Version:** 2.0
**Status:** Current
**Effective Date:** February 1, 2025
**Supersedes:** Version 1.4 (effective January 1, 2023)
**Policy Owner:** Operations

---

## Business Travel
- **Airfare:** Economy class for flights under 6 hours; premium economy for longer flights.
- **Lodging:** Up to $275 per night in major metropolitan areas, $200 elsewhere.
- **Meals:** Up to $85 per day while on overnight business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- **Ground Transport:** Rideshare and rental cars are reimbursable when public transport is impractical.

## Submission
Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense, with itemised receipts for any item over $25.

## Approval
Reports up to $1,000 are approved by the employee's manager; larger amounts require approval from the COO.
