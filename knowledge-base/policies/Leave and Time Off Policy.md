# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-002
**Version:** 3.0
**Status:** Current
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

---

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 1.75 days of PTO per month, beginning on the start date. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

## Holidays
Insurellm observes 10 company holidays per year, plus a company-wide closure between December 26 and December 31.

## Parental Leave
Employees who become parents through birth or adoption are eligible for 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave after 6 months of service. Leave may be taken in one block or two blocks within 12 months of the event.

## Medical Leave
Medical leave is administered in accordance with applicable federal and state law. Requests are made to Human Resources.

## Bereavement
Up to 5 paid days for immediate family and 2 paid days for extended family.
