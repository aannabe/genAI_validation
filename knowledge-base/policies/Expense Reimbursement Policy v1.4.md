# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Document ID:** POL-FIN-004
**Version:** 1.4
**Status:** Superseded by Version 2.0 on February 1, 2025
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2023
**Policy Owner:** Operations

---

## Business Travel
- **Airfare:** Economy class for all flights.
- **Lodging:** Up to $225 per night in major metropolitan areas, $175 elsewhere.
- **Meals:** Up to $60 per day while on overnight business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable.
- **Ground Transport:** Rideshare and rental cars are reimbursable when public transport is impractical.

## Submission
Expense reports must be submitted within 45 days of the expense, with itemised receipts for any item over $25.

## Approval
Reports up to $500 are approved by the employee's manager; larger amounts require approval from the COO.
