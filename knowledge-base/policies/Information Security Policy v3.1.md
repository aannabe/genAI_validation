# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SEC-001
**Version:** 3.1
**Status:** Superseded by Version 4.0 on March 1, 2025
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2023
**Policy Owner:** James Wilson, Chief Technology Officer

---

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how Insurellm protects customer, employee, and company information.

## 2. Access Control
1. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed semi-annually.
2. Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access.

## 3. Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters and are rotated every 90 days.

## 4. Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security team within 72 hours of discovery.

## 5. Third-Party Vendors
Vendors processing customer data must complete a security questionnaire.
