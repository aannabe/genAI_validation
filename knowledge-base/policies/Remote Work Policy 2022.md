# Remote Work Policy

**Document ID:** POL-HR-007
**Revision:** 2022
**Status:** Superseded by the 2025 revision on January 1, 2025
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2022

---

## Eligibility
Following the 2022 restructuring, employees may work remotely or from a retained Insurellm office.

## Home Office Stipend
Employees receive a home office stipend of $50 per month, paid with regular payroll. A one-time equipment allowance of $500 is available within the first 30 days of employment.

## Availability
Employees are expected to be reachable during core hours of 10 AM - 3 PM in their local time zone.
