# Contract with Lakeshore Mutual for Bizllm

**Contract Date:** August 12, 2025
**Contract Number:** BZ-2025-0187
**Parties:**
- Insurellm, Inc.
- Lakeshore Mutual Insurance Group

---

## Terms

1. **Coverage:** Insurellm agrees to provide Lakeshore Mutual Insurance Group with access to the Bizllm platform for commercial underwriting, policy administration, and portfolio analytics.

2. **Duration:** This agreement is effective for 12 months from the Contract Date and renews automatically for additional 12-month terms unless either party provides 45 days' written notice.

3. **Subscription Type:** Lakeshore Mutual subscribes to the **Professional Tier** of Bizllm at **$12,000/month**, invoiced monthly in advance.

4. **Payment Terms:** Payments are due on the first business day of each month. Late payments incur a penalty of 1.5% per month on the overdue amount.

5. **Termination:** Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice. Fees are calculated pro-rata through the termination date.

---

## Features

1. Multi-line commercial underwriting workflows
2. Cyber risk assessment tools
3. Advanced portfolio analytics
4. Broker portal and document management
5. API access for policy administration systems
