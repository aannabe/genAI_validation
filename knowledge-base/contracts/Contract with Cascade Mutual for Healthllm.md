# Contract with Cascade Mutual for Healthllm

**Contract Date:** June 9, 2025
**Contract Number:** HL-2025-0214
**Parties:**
- Insurellm, Inc.
- Cascade Mutual Health Alliance, Inc.

---

## Terms

1. **Coverage:** Insurellm agrees to provide Cascade Mutual Health Alliance with access to the Healthllm platform for member eligibility, claims adjudication support, and care management analytics.

2. **Duration:** This agreement is effective for a period of 24 months from the Contract Date, after which it will renew for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 60 days' written notice of non-renewal.

3. **Subscription Type:** Cascade Mutual Health Alliance subscribes to the **Professional Tier** of Healthllm. Fees are charged in accordance with Insurellm's published price list for that tier at the Contract Date and are invoiced monthly.

4. **Payment Terms:** Invoices are due within 30 days of receipt. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

5. **Termination:** Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written notice if the breach is not cured within that period.

---

## Service Levels

1. **Availability:** Insurellm commits to a monthly platform availability of 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance windows.
2. **Support Response Times:** 4 hours for critical issues, 8 hours for high priority, 24 hours for standard requests.
3. **Maintenance Windows:** Sundays 1 AM - 4 AM EST, with at least 72 hours' advance notice.
4. **Service Credits:** If monthly availability falls below the committed level, Cascade Mutual Health Alliance receives a credit of 5% of that month's fees for each full 0.1% shortfall, up to 25%.

---

## Features

1. Member eligibility and enrollment management
2. Claims adjudication support with configurable rules
3. Care management and risk stratification analytics
4. HEDIS and STAR quality reporting dashboards
5. Provider network directory integration

---

## Support

1. **Account Management:** Michael O'Brien, Account Executive, is the named Insurellm contact for this agreement.
2. **Implementation:** 6-week implementation with a dedicated project manager.
3. **Training:** Up to 25 staff members, with quarterly webinars on new features.
