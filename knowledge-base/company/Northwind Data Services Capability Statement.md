## About Northwind
Northwind Data Services provides data enrichment for insurance claims, including vehicle history, property records, and provider validation. Founded in 2011, Northwind processes more than 40 million records per year for carriers across North America.

## Services
- Claims data enrichment API
- Provider and facility validation
- Batch enrichment for historical claims

## Certifications

- ISO 27001 (in progress)

## Pricing
Volume-based pricing starting at $0.04 per enriched record.
